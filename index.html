✳GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to help you get more from these tools.

What is a WHOIS lookup?+

A WHOIS/RDAP lookup can show public domain registration data, such as registrar, registration dates, and name servers. Privacy rules may hide some fields.

Is this domain lookup free?+

Yes, these browser-based tools are free to use. Public service availability, rate limits, and registry coverage can vary.

Why is some domain information missing?+

Registries publish different data, and privacy protections may redact personal details. A missing result does not necessarily mean a domain is unregistered.

What can DNS records tell me?+

A records map names to IPv4 addresses, AAAA to IPv6, MX routes email, and TXT can contain verification and email-security settings.

Are results always real-time?+

Lookups query external public services when requested. Caching, DNS propagation, rate limits, and provider availability can affect results.
